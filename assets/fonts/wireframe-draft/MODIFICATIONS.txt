Derivative of Comic Neue Bold and Bold Italic under the SIL Open Font License. Renamed Wireframe Draft. Horizontal outlines, advances and horizontal GPOS values scale by 1.02. Four distinct roles use rounded outline expansion of 10 or 34 design units; individual simple glyphs/marks are capped to retain contour counts. Non-mark composites reuse the same expanded base letters, preserving their weight. Detached bold accents move 24 units outward; cedilla/ogonek remain attached. Windows clipping metrics enclose all ink; line spacing metrics remain unchanged. Hint programs removed. Exact modifications and input/output hashes are in derivation.json.
