Derivative of Comic Neue Regular, Bold, Italic and Bold Italic under the SIL Open Font License. Renamed Wireframe Draft. Horizontal outlines, advances and horizontal GPOS values scale by 1.02. Four distinct roles use rounded outline expansion of 10 or 34 design units; individual simple glyphs/marks are capped to retain contour counts. Non-mark composites reuse the same expanded base letters, preserving their weight. Detached bold accents move 24 units outward; cedilla/ogonek remain attached. Version 1.004 fits plus and multiplication from the licensed Regular/Italic outlines to lowercase x-height with a 0.65em advance and 32-unit bold expansion. Role-specific category advances refine letter/digit/punctuation spacing; ordinary and nonbreaking spaces share a 0.75 advance factor. Outlines and inherited positioning of other glyphs are unchanged. Revision 1.003 added U+011E/U+011F using existing G/g and breve components with case-sensitive accents and base positioning. The U+0162/U+0163 cedilla repair from revision 1.002 is retained. Pair positioning, mark attachment and contextual accent behavior are preserved. Windows clipping metrics enclose all ink; line spacing metrics remain unchanged. Hint programs removed. Exact modifications and input/output hashes are in derivation.json.
