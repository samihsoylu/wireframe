# Explore the demo

Choose **File → Open example project** in Wireframe. It opens four editable pages for a fictional studio. Choose **File → Save as…** to keep your own copy.

You can also download [`demo.wireframe`](../examples/demo.wireframe) and open it using **File → Open…**.

Choose a page in the left navigator and explore at your own pace:

- **Landing** — a headline, introduction and two linked actions.
- **Desktop contact** — a desktop form with a multiline message.
- **Phone contact** — the same idea fitted to a phone.
- **Tablet order** — a table, delivery details and order summary.

Use **Fit page** in the toolbar, or Command/Ctrl+0, to bring the whole page into view. The notes on each page suggest a few things to try.

Choose **Wireframe** or **Mockup** in the top toolbar to compare hand-drawn and clean styles. This changes every page in the project; **Undo** restores the previous style.

Double-click the Landing headline, type your own wording and press Return. Try **Undo** and **Redo**. For a multiline message, Return adds a line; Command/Ctrl+Enter finishes the edit. Escape cancels an unfinished edit.

Choose **Present** to follow the page links. On Landing, **Contact us** opens Desktop contact and **Place an order** opens Tablet order. **Back to home** returns to Landing. Select Phone contact in the navigator to explore its layout. **Send message** and **Place order** show example actions only. Press Escape to return to editing.

Save your copy, close it and open it again to continue. The `.wireframe` file keeps the editable pages and their font together. [Getting started](GETTING-STARTED.md) covers creating a project of your own.
