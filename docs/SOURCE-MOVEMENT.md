# Moving component sources

Drag a source row onto another library or one of its source rows. The destination row is highlighted during the drag. The source context menu also exposes Move to library. A selected source carries the selected source rows; library headings are not transferred. Dropping outside the navigator or pressing Escape cancels. A destination that already owns every selected source makes no change. The destination expands and the first moved source becomes active.

Movement transfers ownership and the source's placement, notes, crop, markup and lock. Keep its name and canvas coordinates, including name collisions. Source definitions have project-wide identities, so page instances, alternate instances, nested components and their local overrides keep their references. The new library placement receives a fresh scoped identity. Unrelated library contents and notes remain intact. Preserve explicit paint order when removing and appending owning placements.

The entire transfer is one Undo step. Undo restores the exact previous owners, placements, identities, allocator and editing context; Redo restores the transfer. Duplicate source requests, missing or retained resources, invalid destinations and allocation failures cannot partially transfer a selection. Sources already resident at the destination are skipped within a mixed request.

Capture the project session, editing surface and ownership graph when the pointer is pressed. Reject a stale gesture after any of those change. While the gesture owns input, document shortcuts cannot mutate its captured sources. Hidden navigation, a modal, focus loss and pointer cancellation invalidate the gesture. Quick movement/release batches use the same transaction as ordinary drags. A transient caption follows the pointer; the navigator scrolls near its vertical edges.

Model checks cover shared references, crop/lock/notes, duplicate names, mixed owners/resident sources, explicit paint order, persistence, exact Undo/Redo, invalid requests and exhausted allocation. Application input checks cover ordinary, whole-batch and split-batch drags, Escape, outside release, hidden navigation, blocking dialogs and changed editing context. Native acceptance and its limits are recorded in SESSION-SPEC.md. Physical held cadence and every scroll/multi-selection combination require separately scoped evidence.
